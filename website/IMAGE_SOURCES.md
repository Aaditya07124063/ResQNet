# Image Sources

Registry of every externally sourced photograph on the ResQNet website.
Do not add an image to `public/photos/` without an entry here, and do not
use any image whose license cannot be verified on its source page.

All photographs come from **Wikimedia Commons**. Each one's license and author
were read from the file's Commons metadata (and, for the public-domain files,
the raw license template), not assumed. Downloaded: **2026-10-08**.

## Rules this site follows

- **Real photographs only.** No AI-generated or synthetic images, people or scenes.
- **No claims through imagery.** The pictured people are not ResQNet users and
  ResQNet was not involved in these events. The homepage says so, and every photo
  carries a caption with place, date, credit and license.
- **Changes made:** the files were resized and re-encoded to WebP
  (`public/photos/<id>-<width>.webp`, widths 640/1080/1600/2400 where the
  original allows), with EXIF orientation applied and metadata stripped. They
  were not cropped, retouched or composited. Cropping happens only on screen
  through CSS `object-position`, and the mesh section adds a grayscale filter
  and drawn lines in CSS/SVG.
- **Share-alike:** the resized CC BY-SA files are adaptations and remain under
  the same CC BY-SA license as their originals.
- **GODL-India** permits use and adaptation with attribution to the source
  ministry/department.
- Credits appear next to each photo (`PhotoCredit` in `src/components/ui/Photo.tsx`).
  The data is in `src/content/photos.ts`.

## Considered and rejected

- `Aftermath2015.jpg` (CC BY-SA 4.0): strong image, but its EXIF date (2018) and
  vague description do not confirm it shows the 2015 earthquake. Rejected
  because the context is unclear.
- Rescue photos showing body recovery (yellow body bags) were rejected out of
  respect for the people pictured.
- Pinterest, Pexels and Unsplash images: not used. The Pinterest reference was a
  mood reference only and was not scraped. Commons was chosen because its
  per-file license metadata can be checked.
